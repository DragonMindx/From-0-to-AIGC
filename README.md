This guide is designed for newcomers to generative AI. It provides a basic introduction to AI and AIGC, an overview and comparison of mainstream AI models, practical guidance on setting up simple AI agents, and a hands-on first experience with AI-powered workflows.

The goal is to make AIGC accessible to a broader audience and help users move beyond simply trying AI tools toward integrating them into their everyday study, work, and creative workflows as a practical productivity tool.
